Hi Jason,

Hope the recovery from the cold snap has gone well and Mote is coming out the other side of an eventful spring. Quick thanks again to you and Keri for the OFAV inventory data — that's been really useful for Raine and she and Keri are picking up the thread on the RSE modeling side.

I'm writing about a **parallel ask for *Acropora palmata* data**. Raine and I have spent the past year compiling every published *A. palmata* demographic dataset we can find into a Caribbean-wide synthesis — 17 studies, ~7,800 individual-level records — and using it to build a size-structured Lefkovitch population model. The manuscript is targeting *Coral Reefs* and everything follows PRISMA. It's complementary to the RSE work we've been doing together: the RSE tool needs vital rates to parameterize its restoration scenarios, and this synthesis is where those vital rates would come from for *A. palmata*.

**The gap Mote could fill:** Our individual-level restoration data is currently thin — just Pausch et al. (2018) from CRF and Kuffner et al. (2020) from USGS, about 1,000 fragments total. Most of our data (78%) is from the NOAA tagged-colony program, which skews the results toward natural colonies in the Upper Keys. Mote's *A. palmata* outplant monitoring would be the first large independent restoration dataset in the synthesis — and given Mote's geographic spread (Summerland Key nursery, the Lower Keys outplant sites), it would fill real spatial gaps too.

**What we'd need for A. palmata (separate from the OFAV pull Keri already sent):**
- Colony-level records with a size measurement at each monitoring visit (total colony area, L×W, planar area — any measure works; we handle standardization)
- Alive/dead status at each visit
- Site, dates, and genotype/outplant cohort if available
- Pre- and post-2023 data are both valuable — the heatwave scenario analysis needs both

**What we offer:**
- Co-authorship on the *Coral Reefs* paper for you and anyone at Mote who contributes
- Site-specific population viability analyses for Mote's sites — directly usable as RSE scenarios
- All the compiled Caribbean-wide data and code (open on GitHub)

Raine and Keri have a good working rhythm on the Orbicella side, so I'd propose the same setup: Raine and I handle all the cleaning and standardization, nothing is redistributed without permission, and we share the standardized version back. If it's easier to have Keri run the pull since she already has the OFAV pipeline built, that works too — whatever is least disruptive on your end.

Happy to jump on a call if it's easier than email. I know there's a lot on your plate right now.

Thanks,
